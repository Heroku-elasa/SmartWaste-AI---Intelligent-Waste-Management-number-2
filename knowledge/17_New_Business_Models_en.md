# New Plans: Five Complementary Business Models
Options to cut price risk and capital needs — based on fundraising-guide lessons

Why new models?
Main critique: profit depends on the granule-waste spread; a 15% price drop means a loss.
Thiel: big share of a small market first. Floodgate: 'proprietary power' means not needing to compete — for us, waste supply.
The models below either cut price risk, need less capital, or raise margins.

A) Toll processing
The waste owner (factory, dealer) brings material; we return granule for a fee.
Pro: no waste purchase and no granule price risk; near-zero working capital.
Use: fill idle capacity in year 1 (40% utilisation) — mix 30% toll + 70% own trading.
B) Buying-station network
Small neighbourhood stations buy waste from pickers and residents at a fixed price, sort and bale it.
Franchise: the local partner funds the station; we provide brand, training and guaranteed purchase.
This is the proprietary supply power — the direct answer to Game 1 (waste competition) in the game-theory report.
C) Producer compliance service
Under bylaw Art. 12, producers that don't recycle pay a levy.
Offer: direct contracts with packaging producers to recycle an equivalent tonnage + auditable certificate.
Revenue independent of granule prices — similar to Turkey's ÇEVKO model.
D) Making garbage bags or pipe
After the granule market saturates (year 3+), part of the granule becomes a final product.
Ready customers: the municipality (garbage bags) and farmers (irrigation pipe).
E) Mobile unit for small towns
A truck with a baler and shredder compacts waste from nearby small towns so hauling to the plant pays off.

# Business Plan
A phased chain of plastic-waste-to-granule plants in Iran

Executive summary
Iran generates ~20,000 t of plastic waste per day; only a small share is recycled.
We turn PE/PP waste into washed, melt-filtered granules sold to pipe, bag and film makers.
Start: one pilot plant in Mashhad, capex 31 bn Toman (~$132K).
Technical partner: Pendar Polymer Pishro (Hamidreza Panahandeh) — turnkey line, training, after-sales, output buy-back.
Product and customers
Product: recycled HDPE, LDPE and PP granule; market price 105–135k Toman/kg.
Customers: agricultural pipe, garbage bag, film, pallet and technical-part producers.
Edge: consistent quality through hot washing and melt filtration, regular delivery, long-term contracts.
Revenue model and unit economics
Waste 45k Toman/kg; yield 80%; variable cost 20k Toman per kg of granule.
Small plant: 400 kg/h, 4,800 h/yr, fixed cost 15 bn Toman/yr.

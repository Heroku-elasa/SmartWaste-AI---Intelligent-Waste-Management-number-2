# Marketing and Sales Plan
Winning granule buyers and waste suppliers

Marketing mix
Element
Plan
Product
3 grades HDPE, LDPE, PP with datasheet
Price
10–20% below virgin; 6-month contract discount
Place
Direct sales to factories within 500 km
Promotion
Free samples, Iran Plast fair, website and Telegram bot
Sales funnel
30 target buyers → 15 samples → 6 production trials → 3 contracts (80% of pilot output).
Supply side: 10 scrap dealers, 5 factories with industrial scrap, the waste agency.
Budget
About 2% of revenue (~2 bn Toman/yr) for samples, fairs and digital.

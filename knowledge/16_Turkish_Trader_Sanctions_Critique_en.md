# Critique from a Turkish Trader's View and Sanctions Impact
How much, for what stake and how a Turkish granule trader would engage — under the tightened sanctions of Sept 2026

1. Sanctions and banking situation (Sept 2026)
UN sanctions returned via snapback in Sept 2025; the list names 43 people and 78 entities.
In Aug–Sept 2026 the US launched 'Operation Economic Outcast' and sanctioned Turkey's Golden Global Bank for moving Iranian funds; the Treasury Secretary said more banks may follow.
On 23 Sept 2026 Turkey ordered banks to report and delay transactions of UN-listed persons and firms via MASAK.
Practical result: no mainstream Turkish bank will openly process Iran-linked payments; letters of credit are practically impossible.
US petrochemical sanctions cover Iranian polymer products; recycled PE/PP granule may be viewed the same way. The main risk falls on the Turkish side, not on us.

Conclusion: the plan must run fully domestically, with no dependence on foreign money. The Turkish link — as trial purchases and barter — is an upside, not a pillar. The Turkish letters and deck should shift from 'investment' to 'trade cooperation and purchasing'.
6. Concrete offer to a Turkish trader
Step 1: 25 kg free sample + lab sheet (MFI, moisture, ash).
Step 2: one 20 t trial container, paid after inspection.
Step 3: 6-month contract, 50 t/month at a fixed price.
Step 4: if successful, barter machinery against future granule.
